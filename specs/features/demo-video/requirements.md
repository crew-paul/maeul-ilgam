# Demo video requirements

- Tell the fictional story of a farmer needing assistance and a young adult struggling to find local work, then explain how the prototype can support their conversation.
- Runtime at most three minutes; clear English narration and readable English captions.
- Identify generated human scenes as fictional and distinguish actual captured product screenshots from generated imagery.
- Show the real Meta draft and approved opportunity, explain the Meta models used, and do not suggest jobs or outcomes have been verified.
- Make remaining recording and submission limitations explicit.
